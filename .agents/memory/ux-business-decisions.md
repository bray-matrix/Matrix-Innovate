---
name: UX business decisions
description: Approved intake, ownership and relationship semantics for Innovation Hub.
---

Recommend Initiative-first: develop scope, expected outcomes and business
justification before creating a linked Project. Preserve direct project creation
as an alternative for already-defined work; it is not approved as the preferred
long-term organizational policy.

Creating a linked Project does not require Approved initiative status and does
not authorize funding, production deployment or implementation. Do not introduce
new gates as part of interface simplification.

Working definitions are provisional: Organization is an optional parent company
or corporate entity; Client is an individual Matrix customer/account; Program
groups related projects. Do not enforce a new hierarchy or merge records.

Project Owner is accountable for successful delivery and often also serves as
Project Manager. This is expected and acceptable. Do not add a separate Project
Manager field, invent a project sponsor field, or reinterpret Supporting Owners
as formal sponsors. Assigned resources perform work; executive sponsors provide
leadership sponsorship and help remove obstacles.

**Why:** Brian explicitly approved these boundaries for the usability release
on October 16, 2026; additional project-intake controls await usability testing.

**How to apply:** Use these semantics in labels, guidance and future proposals.
Keep presentation changes separate from authorization, schema and governance
changes. Follow the actual Platform identity model; do not infer personal
assignments from free-text owner names.
