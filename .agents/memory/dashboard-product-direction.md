Preserve the dashboard introduction “Have an idea? Start here.” as the foundation
for business intake, with the guided path primary and defined-idea entry
secondary. The approved usability direction changes entry labels to “Develop an
idea” and “Enter a defined idea” and recommends Initiative-first without a new gate.

**Why:** Brian explicitly confirmed this experience worked well during published-app visual review. The product serves broad business innovation, not only AI prototypes.

**How to apply:** Improve surrounding screens without redesigning this introduction. Never relabel a prototype calculation as a project metric; use actual initiative/project data or remove an unsupported metric. Prototype remains a legitimate existing status and capability, not the headline product model.

## Business audience

Matrix Imaging is a print, mail, and digital communications company with
approximately 180 employees. Innovation Hub serves occasional, often nontechnical
users across executive leadership, sales, account management/customer service,
operations, project management, IT, finance, compliance, and HR.

**Why:** Brian identified confusing terminology and workflows as a significant
concern before expanding the executive pilot. Users must not need software,
API, database, architecture, or complex governance knowledge just to begin.

**How to apply:** Prefer task-oriented guidance and progressive detail without
removing capabilities or rebuilding the underlying architecture. Navigation
hypotheses in assessments are proposals, not approved designs.
